## 1. Executive Summary & Listing Pitch

**TrustPulse AI** is an all-in-one reputation growth and social proof SaaS designed specifically for digital marketing agencies, local service businesses (dentists, clinics, auto shops, law firms, restaurants), and e-commerce founders.

### The Problem It Solves:
1. **Unfair 1-Star Google Reviews**: A single disgruntled customer can drop a local business's 4.9 rating to 4.2, causing up to 30% drop in phone inquiries.
2. **Scattered Social Proof**: Customer praise is scattered across Google, Trustpilot, and emails, leaving websites bare and conversion rates low.
3. **Agency Retainer Opportunity**: Agencies charge $99–$299/mo per local client for reputation management, but enterprise software (Birdeye, Podium) costs $300–$500/mo per location.

### How TrustPulse AI Solves This:
- **Smart QR Funnel Gatekeeper (`/r/[slug]`)**:
  - Customer scans a table tent or counter QR code.
  - If they click **4 or 5 Stars**: Celebrates and prompts 1-click copy & instant redirect to Google Maps / Trustpilot to boost public ratings!
  - If they click **1, 2, or 3 Stars**: The **Negative Review Shield** activates! Opens an empathetic private feedback form directly to the business owner. Zero public stars lost on Google!
- **Universal 1-Line Embeddable Social Proof Widgets**:
  - **Floating Toasts**: *"Sarah Jenkins rated 5 stars: 'Painless cosmetic dentist!' — 4m ago"*.
  - **Wall of Love**: Responsive masonry grid with verified buyer badges and platform tags.
  - **Corner Rating Pill**: Compact badge *"4.98/5.0 from 184+ verified reviews"*.
  - **Universal 1-Line Script**: `<script src="https://.../api/widget/script?id=..." defer></script>` with zero npm dependencies and CORS enabled.
- **PulseMind™ AI Review Auto-Responder**:
  - Analyzes sentiment and synthesizes 3 strategic responses in 1.8 seconds: Local SEO Keyword Gratitude, De-escalation & Resolution, and Customer Loyalty.
- **Agency Multi-Workspace Hub**:
  - Manage dozens of client locations from one login.
  - 1-Click printable monthly client PDF performance reports.
- **Interactive Negative Review Revenue-Leak Calculator**:
  - Front-facing slider tool calculating annual revenue saved by shielding against 1-star review drops.
- **Live Client Website Showcase (`/demo/apex-dental-site`)**:
  - Working live dental clinic site demonstrating the widgets in action for prospective buyers.
